Modified forks must clearly distinguish themselves from the official Mourn
project and must not imply Mourn Team endorsement, subject to applicable law.

truthful statements such as “based on Mourn” may identify the origin. Retain
copyright, attribution and license notices required by the relevant licenses.
These requirements concern confusing branding, not permission to modify or
redistribute code or artwork under the AGPL.
