# MOURN Desktop Roadmap

## Vision

MOURN Desktop aims to be the most privacy-respecting social ecosystem client.

## Guiding Principles

1. **Privacy First** - End-to-end encryption by default
2. **Open Source** - Transparent development, community-driven
3. **Minimal Dependencies** - Security through simplicity

---

## Current Features

### Customizable Profiles

Personal profiles accessible at `https://mourn.bio/{username}` - fully customizable with complete user control.

### The Archives

MOURN's education center featuring short essays on important topics. A space for thought-provoking content.

### The Graveyard

A unique 3D universe where members can leave a message every 14 days that appears as a star. Browse through the constellation of community thoughts and memories.

### Private Messaging

End-to-end encrypted messaging built on the Signal protocol. Server acts as a relay without storing anything. Metadata-minimized, truly private communication.

### Dashboard

Comprehensive dashboard with security features, account management, and quick access to all MOURN services.

---

## Upcoming Features

### Archives Overhaul

Major update to the Archives section with improved organization, search functionality, and new content categories.

### Suites

MOURN's version of groupchats and servers - a flexible system for communities to organize, collaborate, and communicate privately.

### Mobile App

Native mobile applications for iOS and Android, bringing the full MOURN experience to mobile devices.

### Secure Calling

End-to-end encrypted voice and video calling capabilities integrated directly into MOURN.

### Proximity Communication

Bluetooth-based proximity communication system for offline, local-area messaging and connectivity.

### Skills Hub

A platform for discovering new skills and developing current ones. Connect with others, share knowledge, and grow together.

---

## Release Philosophy

We believe in releasing features when they're ready, not on a fixed schedule. Quality and security take priority over deadlines.

Features will move from "Planned" to "In Development" to "Beta" to "Stable" as they progress.

---

## Contributing to the Roadmap

Have a feature idea? We'd love to hear it!

1. Check existing feature requests to avoid duplicates
2. Open a new issue with the `feature` label
3. Describe the problem your feature solves
4. Explain your proposed solution
5. Consider including mockups or prototypes
